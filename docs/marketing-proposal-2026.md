## 1. Product Overview

**NiagaX** is a unified AI Business Operating System built specifically for Malaysian micro-SMEs. It combines Finance, HR, Sales (POS), Operations, and Marketing into one mobile-first platform — replacing spreadsheets, WhatsApp chaos, and disconnected tools.

**Core modules:**
- Finance — invoicing, expenses, P&L, ledger
- HR — payroll, leave, appraisals, onboarding
- Sales — POS, barcode scanning, leads, receipts
- Operations — bookings, products, services, suppliers
- Marketing — content calendar, customer segments, broadcasts
- AI Assistants — one per pillar, answers questions and takes actions

**Payments:** Billplz / Curlec (local, no Stripe dependency)  
**Stack:** Next.js 15, Supabase, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, Vercel (Singapore edge)

## 2. Why We Skip Meta Ads (For Now)

Meta ads require existing brand awareness and proven conversion data to be cost-efficient. We build both first — then ads become a multiplier, not the starting point.

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| No brand recognition yet | Cold ads don't convert for unknown SaaS. Trust must exist before spend. |
| Peer trust drives SME decisions | Malaysian small business owners decide based on other business owners' recommendations, not banner ads. |
| Organic assets compound | Content, community, and affiliates keep working after you stop. Ad spend stops the moment budget stops. |
| Affiliates have built-in trust | A creator with 10k engaged SME followers carries trust that no CPM can buy. |
| Better CAC at this stage | Affiliate referral CAC is 5–10x lower than cold Meta traffic for a new, unknown SaaS. |

> **Rule:** When we do eventually run ads (Month 6+), they will retarget warm audiences already touched by our organic content — not cold strangers.

## 3. Target Audience Breakdown

Never use generic messaging. Each segment has a different pain and demands a different angle.

| Segment | Core Pain | Our Messaging Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **F&B** | Manual stock, daily cash reconciliation, no POS insights | Show POS + inventory + daily profit in 60 seconds |
| **Freelancer** | Chasing invoices, no tax records, scattered income tracking | Invoice to payment + income summary that accountants accept |
| **Small Retail** | Barcode chaos, over/under-ordering, no staff scheduling | Scan barcode → auto restock alert + payroll in one app |
| **Small Agency** | Client billing, time tracking, team HR all in separate tools | One login: invoice client, pay team, track project hours |
| **Solo Entrepreneur** | Everything done manually, zero time for admin | AI assistant handles your admin so you focus on sales |
| **Affiliator** | Want passive income from a tool their audience will actually use | Promote NiagaX, earn 30% recurring commission monthly |

### WhatsApp — P0
**Type:** Owned channel  
**Effort:** Low | **Reach:** High (Malaysia-native)

**Strategy:**
- Build a broadcast list from Day 1. Every trial user, enquiry, and warm lead goes on the list.
- Send daily value broadcasts — tips, mini case-studies, 'did you know' facts relevant to each segment.
- Create a NiagaX Community WhatsApp Group (500-member cap per group; manage overflow with a second group).
- DM funnel: anyone who replies to a broadcast gets a personal follow-up and trial link.
- Use broadcast as the bottom-of-funnel nudge before trial expiry.

**Tone:** Casual, BM + EN mix. Speak like a knowledgeable friend, not a salesperson.

**What to send:**
- Mon: Weekly business tip (segment-specific)
- Wed: Customer story or mini case-study
- Fri: Product feature highlight with demo link
- Weekend: Engagement question ("Korang guna Excel ke untuk kira gaji? Reply la")

---

### Instagram — P0
**Type:** Organic social  
**Effort:** Medium | **Reach:** High

**Strategy:**
- Reels are the primary format. Target: 3 Reels per week minimum.
- Each Reel should demonstrate a specific pain being solved in 60 seconds or less.
- Carousels for education: '7 things NiagaX does that Excel cannot', '5 signs you need a proper POS'.
- Stories daily: polls, Q&A stickers, behind-the-scenes, customer shoutouts.
- Engage: reply to every comment within 2 hours. Comment on SME creator posts with genuine value.
- Use niche hashtags: #usahawanmalaysia, #bisnesmalaysia, #fnbmalaysia, #freelancermalaysia

**Content format breakdown:**
- 60% Reels (demos, pain-points, social proof)
- 30% Carousels (education, comparisons)
- 10% Static posts (quotes, stats, announcements)

**Tone:** Visual-first, relatable, slightly humorous. Show real problems, not polished corporate marketing.

### Facebook Groups — P0
**Type:** Community  
**Effort:** Medium | **Reach:** High (Malaysian SME audience is densely on FB)

**Strategy:**
- Join and actively contribute in the top Malaysian SME/Usahawan Facebook Groups (100k–500k members each).
- Share problem-solving posts — never promotional. Example: "Cara track stok kedai runcit tanpa Excel" then mention NiagaX naturally at the end.
- Create and own the **NiagaX Community** Facebook Group. This becomes your lead nurture pool.
- Post daily in your owned group. Host weekly Q&A sessions live.
- Pin a welcome post with the 14-day free trial link.

**Key groups to join and contribute:**
- Usahawan Muda Malaysia
- Bisnes F&B Malaysia
- Komuniti Freelancer Malaysia
- SME Malaysia (various regional groups)

**Tone:** Educational, peer-to-peer. Write like a community member helping others, not a brand.

### LinkedIn — P1

**Effort:** High | **Reach:** Medium (but high quality — founders, managers, decision-makers)

**Strategy:**

**Tone:** Thought leadership. Honest. Occasionally vulnerable. Never salesy.

## 5. Content Pillars

Every single piece of content maps to one of four pillars. Maintain the ratio. If you drift toward 80% product demos, you will lose the audience.

### Pillar 1 — Pain-Point Education (40% of all content)
Teach first. Never sell first. This is the top of the funnel.

**Content ideas:**
- "5 signs your manual invoicing is costing you customers"
- "Why F&B owners lose 30% margin without real-time stock tracking"
- "Thread: How we ran payroll for 8 staff using WhatsApp (and why we stopped)"
- "Freelancer? Here is why your bank statement is NOT your income report"
- "Kedai runcit owners: here is how much you are losing on shrinkage without a system"

### Pillar 2 — Social Proof & Customer Stories (25% of all content)
Nothing converts like a real person with a real result. Prioritise video testimonials.

**Content ideas:**
- "Customer spotlight: Kedai runcit in Ipoh cuts admin time by 3 hours/day"
- "Before/after: freelancer tracks income + tax in one place for the first time"
- "Real numbers: how one F&B owner finally knew which dish was making money"
- Video testimonial: "Sebelum NiagaX, I spent every Friday night doing accounts"
- Screenshot showcase: user's invoice created and paid in the same day

### Pillar 3 — Product Demos (20% of all content)
Show, don't tell. Keep demos to 60 seconds or less. One feature per video.

**Content ideas:**
- "60-second Reel: create invoice → send → get paid, all from your phone"
- "Story series: AI HR assistant answers 'Boleh ambil cuti tak?' in real time"
- "Carousel: POS + inventory + daily P&L — all synced, zero spreadsheets"
- "Screen recording: how a freelancer logs income and expenses in under 2 minutes"

### Pillar 4 — Founder / Build-in-Public (15% of all content)
Humanise the brand. Share the journey. People buy from people they trust.

**Content ideas:**
- "Why we built NiagaX for Malaysians, not a copy of a US product"
- "Our first 50 users: lessons from selling to small businesses"
- "Transparency post: here is how many trials converted this month (and why some didn't)"
- "We almost scrapped the HR module. Here is what changed our mind."

## 6. Affiliate Program — The Primary Growth Engine

Affiliates are the highest-leverage channel for a new SaaS in Malaysia. They bring pre-built trust, an engaged audience, and social proof that no ad budget can match at this stage.

> A micro-influencer with 10k engaged SME followers is worth more than RM 5,000 in cold Meta spend.

### Programme Structure

**Step 1 — Recruit**
- Target: active content creators in business, SME, lifestyle, and finance niches on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
- Minimum: 5,000 engaged followers (engagement rate > 3%). Follower count alone means nothing.
- Priority: micro-influencers with high trust scores and niche SME/usahawan audiences.
- Approach: personal outreach via DM or voice note. Never use a generic template.

**Step 2 — Commission**
- 30% recurring revenue share for 12 months per referred customer.
- Tier up to 40% at 10 active (paying) referrals per month.
- Payment: monthly via Billplz. No minimum payout threshold.

**Step 3 — Affiliate Toolkit**
Provide every affiliate with:
- Branded demo videos (ready to post, no editing required)
- Caption swipe-files in BM and EN
- Referral links with UTM tracking per affiliate
- Product explainer PDF for their audience
- Private affiliate WhatsApp group for priority support, product updates, and community

**Step 4 — Leaderboard & Incentives**
- Monthly public leaderboard announced on our IG Stories and WhatsApp broadcast.
- Top affiliate each month: featured on our channels + RM 200–500 cash bonus (tiered by referral volume).
- Creates healthy competition and social proof among affiliates themselves.

**Step 5 — Convert**
- All affiliate referral links go to a dedicated landing page with a **14-day free trial, no credit card required**.
- Reducing friction at the trial entry point is the single biggest lever on conversion rate.

| Month | Active Affiliates | Expected Trials from Affiliates |

| Month 1 | 5 | 30 |
| Month 2 | 12 | 80 |

## 7. 6-Month Launch Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Week 1–4)

- [ ] Set up NiagaX Community Facebook Group. Post daily for the first 30 days.
- [ ] Build WhatsApp broadcast list — onboard first 200 contacts via lead magnet (free PDF guide).
- [ ] Create lead magnet: **"Panduan Urus Bisnes Kecil 2026"** — a practical PDF for Malaysian SMEs.
- [ ] Record 5 core short-form videos — one per target segment pain point.
- [ ] Publish founder story on LinkedIn and X. Be vulnerable. Be real. No corporate speak.
- [ ] Identify 10 potential affiliates and send personal voice note outreach (not a template).
- [ ] Set up affiliate tracking links and onboarding flow.
- [ ] Create a content calendar template and commit to posting schedule.

### Phase 2 — Growth Loop (Month 2–3)

- [ ] Launch affiliate programme formally — target 20 active affiliates by end of Month 3.
- [ ] Maintain posting cadence: 5x/week IG + FB, 2x/week LinkedIn, daily WhatsApp.
- [ ] Host monthly free online workshop: **"Urus Bisnes Tanpa Pening Kepala"** — live on Zoom or IG Live.
- [ ] Collect 5 customer stories. Video testimonials preferred over text.
- [ ] Launch SEO blog: 1 article/week targeting Malaysian SME keywords.
- [ ] Partner with 2–3 local accountants or HR consultants for co-created content (they get exposure, you get credibility).
- [ ] Activate affiliate leaderboard. Announce first winner publicly.

### Phase 3 — Authority (Month 4–6)

- [ ] Pitch to BFM, Astro Awani, Tech in Asia, and The Edge — angle: AI is now accessible for Malaysian small businesses.
- [ ] Apply to partner with MDEC, SME Corp, or MyKreativiti for co-branding or co-promotion.
- [ ] Launch user referral: refer a friend → both get 1 month free added to account.
- [ ] Host webinar series with industry guests: local accountant, HR consultant, F&B chain operator.
- [ ] Publish a public **Transparency Report** at 1,000 trial signups — trial numbers, conversion rate, top user segments. This generates earned media.
- [ ] Explore Lazada/Shopee seller community — massive, underserved SME segment with high tool adoption intent.
- [ ] Assess performance data and decide on a controlled paid ad experiment for retargeting warm audiences.

## 9. Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Posting promos without value**  
Every post that is just "sign up now" or "cuba percuma" destroys trust. Lead with help. The sign-up follows naturally when people trust you.

**Inconsistent posting**  
Algorithms and audiences reward consistency over virality. Show up every day for 90 days before judging results. Virality is a bonus, not a strategy.

**Generic messaging to all segments**  
F&B owners don't care about freelance invoicing. Retail owners don't care about agency project tracking. Segment your content the same way you segment your product.

**Ignoring WhatsApp**  
This is Malaysia. WhatsApp is where business decisions are made, recommendations are shared, and trust is built. It must be the backbone of your conversion funnel — not an afterthought.

**No community ownership**  
Participating in other people's Facebook groups is great for discovery. But you must own your own community (your Facebook Group, your WhatsApp group) to control the narrative and capture leads long-term.

**Skipping video**  
Text posts don't convert Malaysian SME audiences in 2026. Short-form video is non-negotiable for Instagram and Facebook distribution. If it's not on video, it's not reaching the right people.

**Hiring a social media executive too early**  
The first 3 months of content must come from the founder or core team. Authentic founder voice is the product. A hired executive will produce polished content that gets ignored.

**Measuring vanity metrics**  
Followers and likes are not revenue. Measure: trial signups, WhatsApp contacts, affiliate activations, and paying users. Report only on those four.
